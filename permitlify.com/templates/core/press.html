Company Overview

About Permitlify

Permitlify is an AI-powered permit intelligence platform that helps home service contractors find and win new jobs using building permit data. The platform monitors 248+ municipal permit portals across the United States daily, enriches each permit record with homeowner contact information, scores every lead using a proprietary AI model (0–100), and delivers a ranked daily feed to contractors' dashboards and email inboxes by 6 AM local time.

Permitlify was founded in 2024 and is based in Fort Worth, Texas. The company serves roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and general contracting businesses across the United States.

Brand Identity

Logo & Wordmark

The Permitlify logo consists of two elements: the document icon (a permit document with a checkmark and location pin overlay, rendered in a blue-to-green gradient) and the wordmark ("Permit" in deep navy #1C2F74, "lify" in green #49C85B, Plus Jakarta Sans 800).

Do not alter, recolor, distort, or add effects to the logo. Always use the logo with adequate clear space equal to the height of the "P" in Permitlify.
