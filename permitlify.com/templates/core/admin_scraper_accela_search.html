Accela Permit Search Finder

Pick a state, edit the city list if you want, then click Search all cities. Each city goes through 3 steps: 1) local web search, 2) DO Inference picks the best *.accela.com URL, 3) local Playwright fetches the chosen page and DO Inference verifies it’s actually a live permit-search page. Only verified URLs are auto-pushed to scrapers. Cities that already exist in your scrapers are filtered out of the city list automatically.
